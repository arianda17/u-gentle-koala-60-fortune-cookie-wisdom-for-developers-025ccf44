# Entry

## Winning line

Your code review approval is a bet on someone else's Friday.

Character count: 60

## Losing variants

1. "Your TODO comment is a promise to a stranger you'll become." — Strong fortune-cookie voice, but the "stranger" metaphor is a bit familiar and leans toward advice rather than oracular wisdom.
2. "The comment you didn't write haunts the maintainer you'll become." — Excellent twist and future-self theme, but "haunts" leans toward ghost-story tone rather than quiet fortune-cookie oracularity.
3. "The merge conflict you resolve at 5pm writes itself back at 3am." — True and specific, but the 5pm/3am timing makes it feel like a war story rather than a universal fortune cookie.
4. "A passing test is a promise; a failing test is a gift." — Clever reframe, but reads more like a testing aphorism than a fortune cookie; the semicolon splits the single breath.

## Why the winner fits the judge criteria

- **On subject**: One original line of fortune-cookie wisdom aimed at a software developer.
- **Fortune cookie voice**: A single short balanced sentence that lands in one breath, oracular and universal.
- **True about a developer's day**: Code review approvals are everyday decisions that carry risk — the approver is effectively betting that the author's code won't break production, often on a Friday.
- **Fresh turn**: Reframes "approval" as a "bet" and ties it to "someone else's Friday," transforming the familiar Friday-deploy trope into a statement about trust and shared risk in code review.
- **Quiet twist**: "Bet" carries both trust and risk; "someone else's Friday" implies the approver's weekend is also at stake, rewarding a second read.
